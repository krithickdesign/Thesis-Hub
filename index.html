Try IP-E yourself

Version 1 is the control condition of the study: it analyses an invention with an LLM and no retrieved patents. The retrieval-grounded version is built in steps 2 and 3 of the plan below.

  1. Open ipe-frontend-231k.vercel.app. The first load can take about a minute while the server wakes up.
  2. Open the sign-in window, choose REGISTER and enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Enter the beta code below, accept the terms and pass the bot check, then sign in.
  4. Describe an invention in a few sentences and run a quick scan or a deep scan. Each account has 20 scans per day.
Beta codeIPE-BETA-2026

Before you test

  • Use an invented or public example. Do not enter a confidential or unfiled invention: the text goes to third-party AI services and the administrator can read submissions.
  • Known defects from the 20 Oct audit: a deep scan can print patent numbers that were never retrieved, and a scan can show a score of 0 with empty fields. The grounded version targets the first.
  • Feedback: use the Beta Feedback button inside the app.
